# Code of conduct

This is a small project. The rules are short.

**Be straight with people.** Say what you mean, including when you disagree.
Disagreement about a design decision is the point of a code review, not a
problem with one.

**Argue about the work, not the person.** "This lets a write through without a
snapshot" is a review. "You clearly do not understand this" is not.

**Assume the other person has context you do not.** They may know the server,
the failure mode, or the reason a thing is the way it is.

**Harassment, personal attacks, and unwelcome attention are not acceptable**,
here or anywhere the project is discussed. Neither is deliberately wasting a
maintainer's time.

## If something goes wrong

Email **arhaankhan7b@gmail.com**. Reports stay private. I will read it, and I
will tell you what I decided to do.

I maintain this project alone, so I am also the person who has to be held to
this. If I am the problem, say so plainly in the issue or the email, and I
will take it seriously.

## Scope

Applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space where someone is
representing the project.
